2. On your device and cookies

WiselyBet uses one cookie, wb_session, which keeps you signed in. It is strictly necessary, cannot be read by page scripts and expires after 14 to 30 days or when you sign out. There are no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking cookies.

Your settings, trackers and other app data are also kept in your browser's local storage so the board loads quickly and works between syncs. A small play-reminder timer (when you started and when you were last reminded) is also kept in local storage on each device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes these copies. The sign-in page loads Google's Firebase sign-in scripts, which follow Google's Privacy Policy.

3. How we use it

  • To provide WiselyBet: your account, your synced board, your plan and the alerts you turn on.
  • To answer feedback and support requests and to fix problems.
  • To keep the service secure, for example by limiting repeated sign-in attempts and blocking abuse.

We do not sell or rent personal information, share it for advertising, or make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on performing our contract with you (running your account and the features you use) and on our legitimate interest in keeping WiselyBet secure and working.

4. Who processes it

We use these service providers. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, the database for your account and saved data, and email delivery.
  • Render: hosting of the website and servers, including request logs.
  • Browser push services (such as Google, Apple and Mozilla): delivery of phone alerts you turned on.
  • Email provider: delivery of feedback and recap emails to our team.

Odds, scores and statistics come from data providers such as The Odds API and ESPN. We request data from them on our servers and do not send them your personal information. When you open a sportsbook link, the sportsbook receives what your browser normally sends and its own privacy policy applies. Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries, using the safeguards the law requires.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our legal rights, or as part of a sale or merger of WiselyBet, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your data.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account data, saved app data, plan details and phone alert subscriptions: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes them from our database straight away.
  • Feedback emails: as long as needed to handle your request.
  • Error reports and request logs: for the limited period our hosting provider keeps logs.
  • Provider backups expire on their own schedules.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: Settings → Your data → Export, or Download my data on the plan page.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Your data → Delete my account, or on the plan page. This is permanent and signs you out everywhere.
  • Phone alerts: turn them off in Settings or in your browser at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or California law), you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict some uses, and to complain to your data protection authority. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California law. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

7. Security

WiselyBet is served only over HTTPS, the session cookie cannot be read by scripts, passwords are never stored in readable form, and repeated sign-in attempts are limited. No online service can be completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Children

WiselyBet is only for adults of legal betting age. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we will delete it.
