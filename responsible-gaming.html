Responsible gaming

Betting should be entertainment, never a way to make money or to escape problems.

Get help now

Talking to someone is free and confidential, and you don't need to be in crisis to call.

  • United States1-800-GAMBLERNational Problem Gambling Helpline. Call or text, 24/7. Chat at ncpgambling.org.
  • In crisis (US)Call or text 988Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, 24/7. If you are in danger, call 911.
  • United Kingdom0808 8020 133National Gambling Helpline (GamCare), 24/7.
  • Ontario, Canada1-866-531-2600ConnexOntario, 24/7.
  • Peer supportGamblers AnonymousMeetings in person and online.
  • AnywhereGambling TherapyFree online support in many languages.

Is betting still fun for you?

Ask yourself honestly. Do you…

  • bet more than you planned, or more than you can afford to lose?
  • chase losses by betting again to win the money back?
  • borrow money, or use money meant for bills, to bet?
  • hide your betting or feel guilty about it?
  • feel restless or irritable when you try to cut back?
  • bet to escape stress, boredom or other problems?

If any of these sound familiar, it is a good time to take a break and talk to one of the services above.

Habits that keep it under control

  • Set a budget before you start and stop when it is gone. Treat it as the cost of entertainment.
  • Set a time limit and take regular breaks.
  • Never chase losses. A loss is the price of the entertainment, not a debt to win back.
  • Don't bet when you are upset, stressed, tired or have been drinking.
  • Remember the odds. Prices include the sportsbook's margin, and no pick, signal or parlay is a sure thing.
  • Keep a balance with friends, family, work and other interests.

Tools in WiselyBet

  • Session reminder: Settings → Play responsibly. Get a gentle reminder after 30, 60 or 120 minutes of use.
  • Take a break: lock WiselyBet on all your devices for 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days. A break can't be ended early, and phone alerts pause until it ends.
  • Quieter alerts: turn off sounds, alert types or phone alerts in Settings.
  • Close your account: Settings → Your data → Delete my account.

Tools at your sportsbook

Licensed sportsbooks offer deposit, loss and time limits, cool-off periods and self-exclusion in their account settings. Many states and countries also run self-exclusion programs that cover every licensed operator. Blocking apps such as BetBlocker (free) or Gamban can stop gambling sites from opening on your devices.

Keep it adults only

You must be 18 or older, or older where the law requires (21 in many US states), to use WiselyBet. If children use your devices, protect your accounts with passwords and consider parental controls.
